# Research

Malan Lab focuses on understanding neural computation, brain dynamics, and clinical neurophysiology using multimodal signals and computational modeling.

---

## 1. Neural Signal Processing

<div class="card">

We analyze neural signals such as EEG, MEG, LFP, and EMG to decode brain states and cognitive processes.

### Key Work:
- Time-frequency analysis (wavelets, multitaper)
- Source reconstruction (MEG/EEG)
- Artifact removal and preprocessing pipelines
- Feature engineering for brain decoding

</div>

---

## 2. Brain Dynamics & Network Connectivity

<div class="card">

We study large-scale brain networks and oscillatory interactions that support motor and cognitive function.

### Key Work:
- Functional connectivity (coherence, wPLI)
- Oscillatory dynamics (alpha, beta, gamma bands)
- Network reorganization in disease states
- Cross-regional coupling (cortico-subcortical loops)

</div>

---

## 3. Movement Disorders & Motor Control

<div class="card">

We investigate neural mechanisms underlying Parkinson’s disease, freezing of gait, and motor impairment.

### Key Work:
- Freezing of gait prediction using LFP/EEG
- Motor error processing (cerebellar-cortical loops)
- Motor adaptation and learning
- DBS modulation effects on motor circuits

</div>

---

## 4. Brain–Computer Interfaces (BCI)

<div class="card">

We develop computational frameworks for decoding brain activity into control signals.

### Key Work:
- Motor imagery EEG classification
- SSVEP-based BCI systems
- Feature selection and dimensionality reduction
- Real-time neural decoding pipelines

</div>

---

## 5. Neural Biomarkers & Clinical Translation

<div class="card">

We identify neural signatures that can serve as biomarkers for diagnosis and treatment monitoring.

### Key Work:
- Alpha power and slowing in chronic pain
- Biomarkers for Parkinson’s disease
- EEG/MEG-based clinical indicators
- Machine learning for neurodiagnostics

</div>
