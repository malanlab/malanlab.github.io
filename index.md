  <!-- ABOUT -->
  <h2>Computational Neuroscience & Neuroengineering</h2>

  <div class="card">
    <p>
      The <strong>Malan Lab</strong> at IIT Jammu investigates how neural
      circuits and large-scale brain networks generate movement, cognition,
      and behavior. We combine computational neuroscience, neurophysiology,
      signal processing, and artificial intelligence to understand brain
      function across health and neurological disease.
    </p>

    <p>
      Our research integrates multimodal neurophysiological recordings
      including EEG, MEG, and deep brain stimulation (DBS) local field
      potentials (LFPs) to uncover neural mechanisms underlying motor
      control, motor learning, chronic pain, and movement disorders.
    </p>

    <p>
      By developing advanced computational tools and neural biomarkers,
      we aim to bridge fundamental neuroscience with next-generation
      neurotechnology and clinical applications.
    </p>
  </div>

  <!-- RESEARCH THEMES -->
  <h2>Research Themes</h2>

  <div class="grid">

    <div class="card">
      <h3>🧠 Brain Dynamics & Neural Oscillations</h3>
      <p>
        Understanding how neural oscillations and large-scale brain
        networks support movement, perception, and cognition.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>⚡ Neuroengineering & Brain Technology</h3>
      <p>
        Developing computational approaches for monitoring,
        decoding, and modulating brain activity.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>🚶 Motor Control & Movement Disorders</h3>
      <p>
        Investigating neural mechanisms underlying Parkinson's disease,
        freezing of gait, motor impairment, and rehabilitation.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>🎯 Deep Brain Stimulation</h3>
      <p>
        Studying cortical-subcortical interactions and mechanisms of
        therapeutic neuromodulation using DBS recordings.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>🔗 Network Neuroscience</h3>
      <p>
        Characterizing functional and directional connectivity across
        distributed brain networks using advanced connectivity measures.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>📈 AI & Neural Biomarkers</h3>
      <p>
        Applying machine learning and computational modeling to identify
        biomarkers for neurological disorders and treatment outcomes.
      </p>
    </div>

  </div>

  <!-- WHY IT MATTERS -->
  <h2>Why This Research Matters</h2>

  <div class="card">
    <p>
      Neurological disorders affect millions of people worldwide and remain
      one of the leading causes of disability. Despite major advances in
      neuroscience and neurotechnology, many neural mechanisms underlying
      disease and recovery remain poorly understood.
    </p>

    <p>
      Our goal is to develop robust computational tools, neural biomarkers,
      and mechanistic insights that improve diagnosis, rehabilitation,
      and personalized neuromodulation therapies.
    </p>
  </div>

  <!-- METHODS -->
  <h2>Methods & Technologies</h2>

  <div class="grid">

    <div class="card">
      <h3>EEG</h3>
      <p>
        High-density electrophysiology for studying neural oscillations,
        sensorimotor processing, and brain dynamics.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>MEG</h3>
      <p>
        Source-space neuroimaging and whole-brain analysis of neural
        activity with millisecond temporal resolution.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>DBS & LFPs</h3>
      <p>
        Recording and analyzing subcortical neural activity during deep
        brain stimulation therapies.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Signal Processing</h3>
      <p>
        Time-frequency analysis, neural oscillations, burst dynamics,
        source localization, and spectral modeling.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Brain Connectivity</h3>
      <p>
        Coherence, phase synchrony, directional connectivity, and network
        neuroscience approaches.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Machine Learning</h3>
      <p>
        Statistical learning, predictive modeling, and computational
        biomarker discovery.
      </p>
    </div>
