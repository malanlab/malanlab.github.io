## About the Lab

<div class="about-grid">

<div>

Malan Lab is a Computational Neuroscience and Neuroengineering research group at IIT Jammu focused on understanding how neural systems generate movement, cognition, learning, and behavior.

Our research integrates multimodal neurophysiology, signal processing, machine learning, network neuroscience, and computational modeling to investigate brain function across multiple spatial and temporal scales.

We develop quantitative approaches for studying neural oscillations, brain connectivity, motor control, and neurotechnology applications in both healthy individuals and neurological disorders, with a particular emphasis on Parkinson’s disease, chronic pain, stroke, and neurorehabilitation.

</div>

</div>

---

## Mission

<div class="card">

To advance our understanding of brain function through computational neuroscience, multimodal neurophysiology, and neuroengineering while developing objective neural biomarkers and technologies that improve diagnosis, rehabilitation, and patient care.

</div>

---

## Vision

<div class="card">

To establish a leading interdisciplinary research program that bridges neuroscience, engineering, artificial intelligence, and clinical neuroscience to address fundamental questions about the human brain and develop next-generation neurotechnologies.

</div>

## Research Areas

<div class="grid">

<div class="card">
<h3>Neural Signal Processing</h3>

EEG, MEG, LFP, EMG, and multimodal physiological signal analysis using advanced spectral, time-frequency, and machine learning methods.

</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Brain Dynamics</h3>

Investigation of neural oscillations, synchronization, functional connectivity, and large-scale network organization.

</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Computational Neuroscience</h3>

Data-driven modeling of neural systems, brain-state transitions, and mechanisms underlying cognition and behavior.

</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Neuroengineering</h3>

Development of tools, algorithms, and neurotechnology platforms for neural measurement, analysis, and intervention.

</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Movement Disorders</h3>

Research on Parkinson's disease, freezing of gait, deep brain stimulation, and motor control mechanisms.

</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Neurorehabilitation</h3>

Biomarker discovery and translational approaches for stroke recovery, chronic pain, and neurological rehabilitation.

</div>

</div>

---

## Current Focus

<div class="grid">

<div class="card">
<h3>Deep Brain Stimulation</h3>

Understanding how DBS modulates neural networks and restores motor function through electrophysiological biomarkers.

</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Brain Connectivity</h3>

Investigating coherence, phase synchronization, WPLI, and network-level interactions across cortical and subcortical regions.

</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>Brain–Computer Interfaces</h3>

Developing computational methods for decoding neural activity and enabling adaptive neurotechnology systems.

</div>
